Step-by-step user flow

From stock entry to profit report — one clean loop.

This is exactly how a pharmacy uses PharmaDesk every day. Steps 04–06 are where competitors stop and PharmaDesk keeps going.

Add medicine stockBuild the shop's medicine database once — name, generic, company, price.
Purchase with batch + expiryEvery purchase entry records batch number and expiry date.
Sell — stock reducesScan or search at the POS; the earliest-expiring batch is sold first.
Low stock warningThe system flags items below the reorder point before shelves go empty.
Expiry warning90 / 60 / 30-day alerts on screen and by SMS. Expired stock is blocked.
Return near-expiry stockOne-tap return notes recover money from suppliers while returns are still accepted.
Monthly sales & profitDaily and monthly reports show sales, profit, and losses prevented.
