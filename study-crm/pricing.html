Quick answers

Subscription or one-time license — which should I pick? If you'd rather avoid a large upfront payment, start on SaaS. If you prefer to own the software outright, the one-time path plus AMC works out similarly over the long run.
What does the setup fee cover? Environment provisioning, initial data import, and getting your counselors set up with roles and logins.
Can I move between tiers later? Yes — talk to sales about upgrading; setup fees are prorated toward the next tier.
